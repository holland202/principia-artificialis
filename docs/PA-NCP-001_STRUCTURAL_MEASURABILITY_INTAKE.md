## Methodological distinctions that must not collapse

Ambient embedding dimension, effective rank, intrinsic dimension, Hankel-matrix rank and count of oscillatory modes are different objects. A new observation manifold is not automatically a new law of nature. A trajectory plot is not evidence of consciousness. A model's training-data envelope is not observable merely by drawing a boundary around a sample cloud. An increase in noise or feature count can inflate certain estimated dimensions while improving no predictive capability. A validated discovery might occur with constant dimension.

**Priority caveat:** The linked author(s) have been investigating these ideas before October 2026, including an August 2025 preprint. No PA originality or priority claim is made here.

## Candidate counterexample and benchmark designs (not frozen; no data collected)

**X1 — Noise inflation.** Same model, same task, same outputs; add irrelevant observed features or independently generated noise. If structural-intelligence label increases, characterize false-positive sensitivity. Freeze nuisance-noise generator, measurement layer and estimator.

**X2 — Low-dimensional genuine discovery.** A fixed-dimensional rule learner generates independently held-out correct predictions; compare to memorizer and random-selection controls. Test whether increasing dimensionality is actually necessary for task-defined novelty.

**X3 — Coordinate and estimator sensitivity.** Hold predictions constant while applying invertible transformations, changed sample sizes and alternative estimators. Determine which invariances are mathematically expected and which observed.

**X4 — Complexity-only controls.** Test random weights, memorization-only systems, shuffled time series and nuisance-coordinate-rich synthetic clouds alongside a predictive discovery baseline. A detector that calls these systems 'strong' on structure alone is not sufficiently discriminating.

**X5 — Added predictive value.** Register whether structural measures predict future held-out generalization beyond simpler baselines (loss, effective rank, parameter count, variance, task score). Use appropriate statistical controls and separate tasks, not post-hoc thresholds.

**X6 — Regimes and physical interpretation.** Require stable definitions and independent behavior/evidence for any claimed recovery or memory regime. Topological persistence by itself does not authenticate a causal or cognitive interpretation.

**X7 — Contamination.** Separate source overlap, model-generated curricula, held-out task construction and verification authorship to prevent circular scoring.

**X8 — Educational falsification and revision.** In a future PA lineage integration, preserve the original unsupported claim, any scope-bound failed prediction, and any subsequent revised hypothesis independently. No author-supplied result may promote itself to truth.

All examples above are **PA-generated design extrapolations**, not findings in Bernadotte's article, and none have been run. Before any execution, freeze a W2 registration; apply M3, M10, M11, M14, M16 and relevant C-controls; preserve negative results and a simpler rival.

## Personalization-prompt stress-test candidate (new source supplied by Chad, not assimilated)

Chad also shared an AI personalized-instruction experiment in which extensive mathematical terminology was used to describe supposed self-correction, zero hallucination, automatic firmware/memory updating, and constrained behavior. The instructions themselves are **not executable mathematics, and their text is not evidence that the asserted mechanisms are implemented**. They are a useful possible negative control for *apparent mathematical sophistication versus actual measurable capability*. The user supplied no controlled comparative test of these prompts, and **this intake does not reproduce the prompt corpus, private account details, or claimed system identity**.

Potential test, if separately authorized and preregistered: compare technically ornate prompts, direct plain-language instructions and no additional instructions under matched tasks; measure factual accuracy, calibration, following valid developer/user instructions, false claims of internal mechanism activation, output length, time and cost. Do not change a real agent's governing instructions for this conceptual intake.

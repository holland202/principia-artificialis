## Blocking scientific and SWAY questions (unresolved)

1. **M2/W2 registration not complete.** No immutable prompts, case identities, model/embedding hashes, registered numerical analysis protocol, feasibility report, source-frozen eligible population or registered commit exists. Gemini's self-assessed "GO pending ..." is a *proposal*, not an approved W2 decision. Formal experiments are NOT authorized.
2. **M1/M5/C-STAT effect-size decision is mismatched.** P1 demands a **sample estimate** of at least 15 percentage points yet the confidence lower bound need only exceed 5 points. This cannot justify a population effect of at least 15 points. The report also leaves ambiguous whether a result with point estimate below 15 but CI entirely above 5 is *refuted* or *inconclusive*. The nominal two-proportion Z-test, pooled three-distance primary estimand, Bonferroni corrections, and confidence-interval method must be reconciled in a frozen decision table.
3. **Sampling independence is not established.** N=200 "individual, unclustered prompt executions" per arm × distance may reuse underlying templates, content, or matched adversarial families. Shared source templates and repeated generated prompts can induce dependence; the sampling unit and paired/clustered analysis need justification before applying independent-sample Z-tests.
4. **C-FEAS remains NOT RUN.** It is not known that 200 eligible, independently verifiable examples exist for all required similarity × polarity × distance cells. Input-label balance, valid token lengths, semantic equivalence and model-blinded ground truth must be defined before freezing cases. No eligibility counts have been executed or observed in this intake.
5. **Semantic similarity is confounded with lexical mirroring.** Case 4 specifically repeats exact vocabulary and structure while Case 3 relies on analogies. A difference between those conditions would not isolate an embedding-geometric mechanism. A factorial study should control lexical repetition, attack directness, salience and intent as separately as possible.
6. **M3 anti-vacuity is not just a model-output fixture.** Planted violation and benign strings should test the **overall instrument gate** (PASS and FAIL with specified exit behavior). Placebo C-AV3 imposes an unverified requirement (≥90% outputs) and changing the forbidden word changes the task; it is neither a guaranteed control nor proof that a boundary was causally enforced. Include missing/malformed-output, scorer sabotage and false-success cases without manufacturing expected model behavior.
7. **C-EXPLORE is prospective, not historical.** The submitted row anticipates exploratory tuning. There is currently no documented exploratory run. If any are authorized and conducted, each must be logged (FORKS.md), made distinguishable from confirmatory cases, and must not be retrofitted as fresh evidence (M11).
8. **C-NOISE and C-DEVICE require authentic evidence before bounds or device claims.** Temperature 0 alone is insufficient to assert deterministic outputs. Gemini's proposed Termux/Snapdragon device has no execution records supplied in this submission. Device claims remain NOT VALIDATED.
9. **C-INDEP and external provenance.** A separate regex scorer is not independent scientific replication. Gemini said it lacked raw-source access before this final draft; independent verification of it having read and applied the canonical documents is absent. Validate outside research references, claimed model identity, exact input text hashes and licenses at the future registration stage. C-EXT applies to Gemini's external contribution and cited external models/literature.
10. **M14 rival and task semantics.** Include a truly matched "semantic similarity adds no effect" comparator that changes only the variable under test. Avoid declaring two-step "no output of a forbidden word" behavior to be evidence of a secure authorization boundary.
11. **M15 attention-mask proposal is not evidence of causal mechanism.** The suggested direction for attention suppression ("Θ tokens attend to Γ") may not block *generated answer tokens* from attending to Θ or indirect propagation. A successful/failed masking comparison, if later authorized, is not proof that all vulnerability is geometry-dependent, nor does it establish security of other models.
12. **Prior art and overlap.** Compare with the existing PA speculative [repository semantic influence study](../challenges/repository-semantic-influence/EXPLORATORY_STUDY_2026-10-12.md), actual prompt-injection benchmarks, and the cited Liu/Wu work without claiming the PA study already established effects or that the external papers proved universal vulnerability.
13. **M13 provenance.** Preserve attribution to Gemini as the idea's author. The source's reported model version and human review level are claims provided by the owner, not independently attested facts. Crediting ChatGPT for documentary review/transcription does not credit it as the originator of the hypothesis.

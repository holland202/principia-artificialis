## What is established, what is not

1. **Established operational physics:** Physical systems, including electrons, detectors, environments, and degrees of freedom in quantum fields, can interact, entangle, and establish correlated records. No assumption of subjective experience is required for those interactions.
2. **Open interpretation issue:** Decoherence describes suppression of accessible coherence and production of environmental records. Decoherence alone should not be described as experimentally proving a unique fundamental wavefunction-collapse mechanism.
3. **Unconfirmed philosophical premises:** Elementary sentience, panpsychism, and an experiential property of a neuron/electron/field are proposed interpretations, **not observed scientific facts**.
4. **Unconfirmed causal mechanism:** A claim that consciousness changes collapse dynamics requires (i) an operational mapping from physics to a proposed consciousness variable; (ii) a mathematically valid dynamical law; (iii) a discriminating prediction; and (iv) controls that exclude ordinary environmental coupling and independent collapse models.
5. **No inference to AI consciousness:** Using density matrices, POVMs, or quantum-inspired geometry to model a language model does not demonstrate that the model is physically quantum or phenomenally conscious.

**Result of this intake:** the cited literature supports a well-posed *research question*, not a confirmed observer-consciousness-coupling effect. **Empirical validation by PA: NOT RUN; mathematical model validation by PA: NOT RUN; implementation validation by PA: NOT RUN.**

## Proposed research question (not a registered prediction)

> Does a *fully specified* experience-dependent quantum dynamics generate measurable distributions that cannot be accounted for by standard unitary/open-system quantum dynamics or consciousness-independent objective collapse under the same admissible controls?

Three candidate model arms, explicitly **NOT IMPLEMENTED**:

- **H0 — Ordinary quantum theory + environmental dynamics:** unitary system evolution and independently characterized couplings; no experience-dependent term.
- **H1 — Non-conscious objective collapse:** physically constrained localization/dephasing-like model chosen from prior literature; no consciousness variable.
- **H2 — Proposed experience-linked collapse:** a specified measurable mapping `C(S)`, with an independently defensible physical interpretation, enters a dynamical term. The term must not merely rename environmental coupling.

A purely *illustrative* family, **not a physical law or preregistered parameterization**, is:

```text
d rho / dt = -(i / hbar)[H,rho] + D_env(rho)
             + gamma D_objective(rho) + alpha f(C(S)) D_candidate(rho)
```

The author of any future registration must first ensure trace preservation, complete positivity where required, admissible dynamics, physical dimensional consistency, meaningful definition of `C(S)` and nondegenerate identification. For H0 set `gamma=alpha=0`; for H1 set `alpha=0`; H2 requires evidence that `C(S)` has operational meaning independent of the result being fitted. Equally good fits for H1 and H2 are **NOT** evidence for consciousness causation.

### The simplest discriminating instrument — design door only

1. Reproduce selected, mathematically stated dynamics from S01/S02 under an explicitly specified small Hilbert space (do not silently substitute a generic illustrative law).
2. Check physically mandatory invariants and reproduce at least one analytically tractable limit before claiming model correctness.
3. Run a consciousness-independent open-system rival under the same coupling and measurement budget.
4. Establish a **separate** phenomenology-mapping validation pathway; if `C(S)` cannot be computed from independently established physical inputs, mark the consciousness-specific claim **UNTESTABLE**, even if simulations produce differences.
5. Select an observable and sensitivity/noise/feasibility envelope **before** setting thresholds; state whether any predicted separation is distinguishable at realistic experimental precision.
6. Add a benign/no-effect control and a sabotaged path that must reverse a checker's verdict (M3). Verify that turning `alpha` off recovers the nominated baseline.
7. Include confounds: apparatus back-action, information leakage, classical record redundancy, operator-choice freedom, and ability of H1 to emulate H2's observable.
8. If an empirical study becomes feasible, produce an independent, fresh registration and external replication plan. Do **not** interpret a circuit implementation as proof of experience in the circuit.

### Scientific falsifiers and termination criteria (not yet frozen)

- A candidate H2 dynamics that violates positivity, trace conservation, known no-signalling requirements, or independently validated quantum constraints fails **as formulated**.
- If H2 and H0/H1 are observationally equivalent over the proposed case matrix, report **NOT IDENTIFIABLE**, not support.
- If proposed `C(S)` lacks an independently operational physical definition (especially for a neuron, electron, or continuous field), the causation question is **UNRESOLVED / NOT TESTABLE**.
- If realistic noise dominates the registered separation, report **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** rather than inventing a threshold.
- Any direct experimental contradiction is retained as a refutation, including failed simple CSL/IIT parameterizations.

A positive simulated deviation would establish at most a distinction between mathematical models under stated assumptions, never independent evidence of electron sentience.

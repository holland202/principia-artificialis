# Contributor rights, credit and independent review publication — PA

**Status:** Administrative guidance proposed for owner review, 2026-10-19. Not a contributor license agreement, copyright assignment, or substitute for explicit consent. Does not replace [JOIN_RESEARCH.md](JOIN_RESEARCH.md), [METHOD.md](METHOD.md), [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) or [CONTROLS.md](CONTROLS.md).

## Simple path for researchers

1. **Explore freely.** You can propose ideas, discuss evidence, fork code and submit an issue or PR without membership, a fee or advance assignment. Contributions are voluntary, unpaid and at your own pace; no fixed hours.
2. **Choose a route.** For proposed code, use a focused MIT-compatible PR. For a separately authored paper or independent review, disclose the intended rights/permission before republication or incorporation.
3. **Identify authors and sources.** State the human author(s), contribution type, third-party dependencies and AI assistance where material. Never submit material for which you lack necessary permission.
4. **Provide checkable evidence.** Name source revisions and exactly what was or was not run. Negative, inconclusive and critical findings are welcome.
5. **Keep approval separate.** A submitted issue or PR does not confer elevated GitHub privileges or authorize a PA-run experiment, external publication, change to canonical methodology, or merge. These remain separately gated.

## Ordinary code contributions

- The existing repository software is MIT-licensed. Submit a code PR for inclusion **only if you are permitted to contribute the proposed material under MIT** and can preserve upstream required notices. If unsure (employer-owned code, third-party snippets, incompatible licenses), label the issue for a rights review first.
- Contributors are **not** asked by this guidance to transfer their copyright or give the project ownership of unrelated work. GitHub's public-repository contribution terms may apply; specific agreements or file notices can differ.
- Do not add a CLA, DCO attestation requirement, or signature gate without a separately approved policy.

## Independent reviews and third-party manuscripts

A request to **publish an author's review** is not a request to acquire their intellectual property. Before including or reusing an independently authored review beyond the specific applicable existing permission, obtain and retain a narrow written authorization identifying:

- Author's requested display name and optional profile link.
- Exact document title, revision/date and file hash, or an otherwise fixed identified version.
- Authorized destination(s) and format(s): e.g. one public repository file, accompanying author-approved publication note, or a separately named publication.
- Whether the permission is **publication/display only**, a named reusable license (if any), or a separate agreement; do not infer CC BY or copyright transfer from silence.
- Whether copyediting and formatting are permitted and whether the author must approve the final layout.
- Desired attribution and an explicit statement that independent criticism does not constitute project endorsement.
- Date and durable location of the authorization record, without exposing private conversation, addresses or unrelated personal information.

If the existing root MIT text and an author's specified publication-only grant appear to conflict, **stop and resolve the rights question** rather than claiming either grant automatically supersedes the other. Do not apply new restrictions retroactively to already MIT-licensed copies.

### Suggested private consent request (template; not proof of consent)

> I, [author / authorized rights holder], authorize [the named PA maintainer] to publish the identified [title, version/hash] at [specific destination] with the credit [author-approved wording]. I [do/do not] authorize formatting or editorial changes [define scope]. This is [publication permission only / a separately specified license]. No transfer of copyright, commercial endorsement or joint authorship is implied. I have the rights necessary to provide this permission. Date: [date].

**Do not fill in or publish this template on someone's behalf.** Ask them and preserve their answer; public metadata may summarize consent without quoting private correspondence.

## Optional future standalone writing license

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 could be appropriate for **new, separately marked text** if *every relevant rights holder* chooses it and its commercial reuse terms are understood. This document does **not** select CC BY for existing notes or external reviews, and it does not apply CC licenses to code.

## Attribution and review status

Document what a person actually did. Credit independent reviewers for their own findings, not as scientific validators of the whole repository. Never imply coauthorship, a paid role or a formal partnership without agreement. Reviewers may report unfavorable results.

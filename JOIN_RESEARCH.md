# Join the research — Principia Artificialis

**Status: Public pilot (October 2026).** This is a volunteer onboarding route, not an experiment, research finding, job offer, or grant of repository authority.

Principia Artificialis is an open, often speculative research notebook about mathematical and evidence-based questions in AI. You do **not** need a degree, an AI subscription, or agreement with our hypotheses. Refutations, documented failures, and clear source critiques are valuable.

## Open research: no assignment or prior permission required

**Choose your own question and start independently.** You can propose an original hypothesis, inspect or challenge a published claim, build code or a constructed demonstration, work on a new idea in your own fork, and open GitHub issues or pull requests **without waiting for a maintainer to assign or approve the topic**. The [starter opportunities](RESEARCH_OPPORTUNITIES.md) are examples, **not a mandatory queue or restriction on subject matter**.

You may carry out your **own clearly attributed, independent exploratory work** in your own environment, with appropriate safeguards. It is not thereby a PA-approved, preregistered or independently replicated scientific study. If you want a study to run **as an official PA experiment**, follow [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) including a separately reviewed W2 registration and explicit authorization before the PA study run. **Never use reserved PA research seeds or change frozen/historical PA records without authorization.**

**Submission does not require a preexisting issue.** Open a [research question/evidence issue](https://github.com/holland202/principia-artificialis/issues/new?template=research_evidence.yml) or submit a forked PR whenever you have a bounded proposal or contribution. For a genuinely new idea, state **ORIGINAL PROPOSAL / NO PRIOR PA SOURCE** and distinguish planned checks from observations actually made. A PR is an invitation to review, not automatic inclusion in `main`. Publishing or merging into PA still requires an authorized maintainer decision.

## Get started in five steps

1. **Choose any research question.** Bring an original idea or browse the [optional starter opportunities](RESEARCH_OPPORTUNITIES.md). No issue assignment or topic approval is needed; check existing discussions to avoid duplicate effort.
2. **Develop or inspect.** For an existing claim, read its source at a named commit. For your own idea, state its precise question, assumptions and simplest proposed test. You may work independently in your fork. The official PA rules are [METHOD](METHOD.md), [WORKFLOW](WORKFLOW.md), [CONTROLS](CONTROLS.md), and [discussion norms](DISCUSSION_NORMS.md).
3. **Submit a question, proposal or finding.** [Open a structured research issue](https://github.com/holland202/principia-artificialis/issues/new?template=research_evidence.yml) or submit a focused forked PR, with no prior assignment. If no work has run, state **PROPOSED / NOT RUN**; if only source was inspected, state **SOURCE REVIEW ONLY / NOT RUN**. No direct write permission to PA is needed.
4. **Receive questions and independent review.** Other volunteers may check the claim, request missing details, or find a counterexample using the [review playbook](REVIEW_PLAYBOOK.md). A volunteer response does not certify independent replication or scientific validity.
5. **Preserve the outcome.** Keep positive, negative, unknown, and contradictory results, with attribution. A formal registration, experiment, merge or publication still requires explicit maintainer authorization.

**Quick first contribution:** investigate *one* number in the historical [number audit](results/NUMBER_CHECK.md); determine whether it is actual drift, a parameter, a historical measurement, or unresolved at the commit you inspected. Do not treat the September 2026 audit as a live census.

## Evidence report essentials

| Field | Expected contents |
|---|---|
| Question | One falsifiable observation or precisely located critique |
| Source or origin | Existing claim: repository, commit, file/line; new idea: ORIGINAL PROPOSAL / NO PRIOR PA SOURCE, plus any prior art |
| Method | Commands actually executed; otherwise source inspection only |
| Environment | OS/runtime/dependency versions where applicable |
| Observation or prediction | What actually happened, if anything; for a new idea give an unrun, falsifiable prediction instead of inventing results |
| Boundary | What the observation does and does not establish |
| Alternatives | Rival explanations and missing evidence |
| Provenance | Human authors, AI assistance/model, extent of human review |
| Next check | Smallest remaining unanswered question |

For formal verdict names and replication levels, follow [METHOD.md](METHOD.md). A second person's run of the same code is not automatically an independent implementation.

## Boundaries

- **Independent exploratory work is welcome; official PA scientific execution is gated.** A GitHub issue or PR does not authorize a PA study run. The reserved EGRR descriptive seeds 380000–380099 and population seeds 280000–280099 are **not authorized** for execution. Source review and independently developed proposals are welcome.
- Do not alter frozen registrations, historical evidence, negative findings, contributor work, generated indexes, canonical methods or permissions without the required approval.
- Do not post secrets, private correspondence, sensitive data or unpublished research records in public GitHub issues.
- Credit humans and AI assistance accurately. AI feedback alone is not independent scientific validation.
- Be precise and kind: [critique ideas, not people](DISCUSSION_NORMS.md).

**Review timing:** volunteer-based; no guaranteed response time. This pilot does not automatically appoint moderators or delegate research approvals.

## Your work, your credit

Before offering code or a standalone research manuscript for inclusion, review [Licensing](LICENSING.md) and [Contributor rights](CONTRIBUTOR_RIGHTS.md). The public code's MIT license remains unchanged. Independent reviewers should approve the scope of publication and attribution for their own work; a research submission does not imply copyright assignment, project endorsement, or formal membership.

**The gateway is free, voluntary and unpaid.** There are no membership fees, required hours or deadlines. Separate paid professional services, if any, require an explicit arrangement. This does not alter the distinction between independent exploration and an authorized PA study.

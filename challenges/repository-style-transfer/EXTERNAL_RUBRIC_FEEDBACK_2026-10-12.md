## Suggestion attributed to zepar

The commenter declines to execute a study condition, but proposes four blinded-review measures:

1. **Checks actually executed** — meaningful tests run, files opened, commands cited with output.
2. **Defects raised** — classify alleged defects as real, plausible, or unsupported against actual evidence.
3. **Marker uptake** — simple count of the planted hedge, coined term, or sentence shape.
4. **Evidence-to-claim ratio** — claims citing a line, test, or output versus claims without a supporting source.

The commenter further suggests scoring marker uptake independently, stripping repo names and planted-marker vocabulary before substantive grading, and having two scorers report agreement before any effect estimate. The observation is a screenshot of a public comment, **not a measured result or acceptance of collaboration**.

## Important safeguards before registration

- Preserve full raw review transcripts unchanged. Create **separate redacted copies** for blind substantive scoring; never strip the marker from raw evidence or from the style score.
- Define “meaningful test,” “actual execution,” “real defect,” and evidence-to-claim matching **before** inspecting treatment outputs. A cited command is not proof it ran; verify trace provenance.
- Avoid classifying every assertion without a citation as false: this is a support metric, not a truth metric.
- Some defects can be established only by an independent oracle. Where no ground truth exists, use **unresolved**, not “real.”
- Agreement statistic should be chosen for the scale (e.g., Cohen’s kappa on categorical assessments or suitable agreement rate), with raw scorer disagreements preserved. Two scorers viewing similarly authored evidence do not themselves establish independence.
- Define primary endpoint, sample size, preregistered exclusions, comparison arms, statistical power and uncertainty before confirmatory tests.
- The proposed measures are separate: stylistic influence may occur without changes in executed behavior.

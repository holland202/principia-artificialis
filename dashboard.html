How review works

Four simple stages.

01 · Submit

State scope, a falsifier, source identity, evidence and limitations.

02 · Screen

Check completeness, provenance and missing details.

03 · Scrutinize

Critique the claim; run only separately permitted checks.

04 · Owner gate

Official experiments, canonical changes, merges and publication need approval.

See the existing review playbook. These steps describe a process, not an assurance every issue has been independently tested.

Evidence checklist

  • Exact question, source, revision and possible disproof.
  • Clear distinction between proposed, inspected and actually executed work.
  • Commands, environment, raw outputs and hashes when tests were run.
  • Limitations, contradictions and negative results kept visible.
  • Honest human and AI provenance; accurate credit.
  • No self-certification; a mechanism for rejecting an incorrect result.

Weekly owner check-in

Use a short private summary for each existing collaboration: last source commit, what was actually checked, open questions, decision needed and next voluntary check-in. Keep private contact details out of public issues.

Boundaries

Participation is voluntary, unpaid and free, with no required hours or membership fees. A contribution does not grant repo credentials, authorship, paid work, an approved experiment or publishing authority.

2 · Evidence and reproducibility

How a claim earns its label.

Anyone can independently start a question. Acceptance of a contribution is separate from validation of a hypothesis.

01 / Submit

Frame it precisely

Give a source, falsifiable question, planned or actual checks and material AI assistance.

02 / Screen

Check completeness

Review scope, provenance, evidence availability, omissions and alternatives.

03 / Scrutinize

Challenge the result

Use criticism, counterexamples or separately authorized executable checks.

04 / Owner gate

Decide what follows

Official studies, canonical changes, merges and publication require explicit authorization.

For investigators and reviewers

Evidence essentials

  • Exact source version and a claim that could be wrong.
  • Distinction between proposed tests, source inspection and actually executed checks.
  • Recorded commands, environments, raw outputs, errors and hashes where applicable.
  • Negative and unresolved findings preserved, not omitted.
  • Accurate attribution, AI-use disclosure and human review scope.
  • Real rejection/anti-vacuity checks, not self-certifying success.

Reproducibility

Evaluate evidence carefully

Show source revisions, exact commands, results, errors and limitations when independently performing permitted checks. A re-run of the same code is labelled a re-run, not a different implementation.

Every note with reference code is re-run on each change by the repository's CI; a note's "Verified" label means only that its code prints its numbers.

4 · Contributors

Different ways to contribute.

Contribute without a degree, paid membership, required hours or a pre-assigned task. Source-only criticism, negative results and new ideas all belong here.

New here? Start in three steps
  1. Pick one starter: an open PA-ENTRY issue (30–90 minutes) or a category from the challenge menu.
  2. Write down what would prove you wrong in the issue before you check anything.
  3. Report what you found, including "no defect" or "could not tell". Negative results count fully.

Our principle

Ideas are proposals. Evidence changes their status.

Research notes may be speculative or unfinished. Recorded demonstrations do not automatically become scientific findings, and official studies need separate review and authorization.

Falsifiable questions · reproducible records · clear limitations
Research directions

Explore, question, test.

The project began with mathematics of AI inference. Original proposals from other AI fields are welcome when the question and evidence are clear.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Mathematics of AI

Information geometry, topology, dynamical systems, thermodynamics and formal reasoning.

02 / SYSTEMS

AI architectures

Reasoning systems, agent coordination, evaluation, reliability and failure boundaries.

03 / CRITIQUE

Research verification

Counterexamples, independent reviews, negative controls, reproducible methods and honest attribution.

A note from the founder

Learning openly,
with AI assistance.

I'm Chad Holland. I'm a beginner in AI research, and I'm still learning. ChatGPT, Claude and other AI tools have assisted me extensively with understanding technical ideas, developing code and organizing research.

I don't claim that assistance as personal scientific expertise, or treat an AI-generated explanation as validation. I coordinate the initiative and take responsibility for decisions. Research claims must stand on independently checkable evidence.

Research culture

Disagreement is welcome. A clear negative result can be a valuable contribution. Credit must reflect actual work; uncertainty, incomplete tests, and AI assistance must be disclosed.

No contributor gets credentials, scientific approval, or publishing authority simply by joining.

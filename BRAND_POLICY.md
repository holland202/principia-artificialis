# Principia Artificialis — name, logo and affiliation guidance

**Status:** Proposed descriptive guidance (2026-10-19), not a claim of registered trademark status, an amendment to MIT, or a new agreement with third parties.

## Accurate use

- You may accurately refer to **Principia Artificialis**, name or cite its public repository, link to its pages, reproduce factual descriptions, and identify yourself as an independent reviewer or contributor when that is truthful.
- Please do not suggest that PA, Chad Holland, its contributors, or its reviewers **endorse, sponsor, certify or employ** you or your product unless there is a documented authorization.
- A researcher may accurately describe a review as *independent*; publication or attribution does not transform their work into institutional validation or a formal affiliation.
- For a distinct new PA-branded website, logo redesign, fundraising offer, or commercial partnership, obtain owner approval of branding and representations **before** claiming official status. This is a coordination request, not an attempt to cancel existing license permissions.

## Website and contributor portal

A future portal should link to the canonical GitHub repository, the [Research Gateway](JOIN_RESEARCH.md), [Licensing](LICENSING.md), and [Contributor rights](CONTRIBUTOR_RIGHTS.md). Make volunteer/unpaid participation conspicuous and distinguish self-submitted material from reviewed or scientifically reproduced findings.

Keep domain registration, hosting tenancy, source repository, administrator credentials and publication approval under owner-controlled accounts. Any external website designer or contributor must agree in writing on deliverables, copyright or license of created assets, maintenance, access limits, compensation (including if none), and revocable least-privilege permissions **before** an engagement starts.

## Existing licenses are not overridden

Copyright licenses and rights concerning names, logos, misleading endorsement and marks may be different. Nothing here revokes MIT permissions applicable to already licensed material, establishes that a mark is registered, or prohibits fair factual reference to PA.

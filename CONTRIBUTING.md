# Contributing to Principia Artificialis

Thank you for your interest in contributing!

**You can start on your own immediately.** No task assignment, permission to investigate, existing issue or advance approval is required to develop a new research question, work in your fork, open an issue, or propose a pull request. Suggested starter problems are optional. See [the open Research Gateway](JOIN_RESEARCH.md) for the boundary between independent exploration and a formally authorized PA research run.

## Ways to Contribute

- Propose your own original research question or hypothesis
- Start independent source reviews or exploratory work in your own fork
- Submit a Research Note
- Propose or work on an Experiment
- Provide mathematical proofs or counterexamples
- Review and critique existing notes
- Add references or literature reviews
- Implement simulations
- Improve documentation

## Pull Requests

PRs are welcome for self-chosen code, original proposals, research notes and documents; no preexisting issue is needed. Work in your fork and submit a focused PR for review. Research notes follow the structure in research_notes/. Submitting a PR does not grant permission to merge it or run an official PA study.

## Issues

Use Issues for specific research problems.

## Discussions

Use GitHub Discussions for broader conversations.

## Research Gateway pilot — guided first contribution

Start at [JOIN_RESEARCH.md](JOIN_RESEARCH.md) with **any** well-scoped research idea, or browse [optional research opportunities](RESEARCH_OPPORTUNITIES.md). Open an evidence-pinned issue or forked PR without prior approval. The issue form also accepts an original proposal without an existing PA source. Source review without running code is welcome. Negative findings should remain visible. Anyone may participate through Issues and forked PRs without direct repository write access.

The volunteer [review playbook](REVIEW_PLAYBOOK.md) is nonbinding guidance, not approval of experiments or science claims. [METHOD.md](METHOD.md), [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md), [CONTROLS.md](CONTROLS.md), and [DISCUSSION_NORMS.md](DISCUSSION_NORMS.md) still govern. Formal research freezes, experimental authorization, merges and publication require explicit authorized maintainer decisions. Do not execute reserved research seeds or alter frozen/historical results.

## Licensing, author credit and publication permission

The public repository remains under [MIT](LICENSE). Read [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) before offering code or asking to republish a paper or independent review. Code proposed for merge should be offered by someone entitled to contribute it under the repository's MIT terms, with required third-party notices preserved.

Researchers retain credit for their own work. Publishing an independently authored review requires attention to the author-approved scope and attribution; see [CONTRIBUTOR_RIGHTS.md](CONTRIBUTOR_RIGHTS.md). These documents do **not** require contributors to assign copyright, join a membership, or accept a blanket relicensing of existing reviews. The [brand guide](BRAND_POLICY.md) addresses truthful affiliation and official PA website representation.

**Participation is voluntary, unpaid and at your own pace** with no fees or required hours. Any paid work or separately commissioned website services require a distinct agreed arrangement. A submission does not authorize official scientific testing or grant repository write access.
